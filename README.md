🎓 Milioner Znanja

Milioner Znanja is an educational quiz game inspired by the popular TV quiz format. Players answer questions from a wide range of categories, test their knowledge, earn rewards, and track their progress while learning in a fun and engaging way.

✨ Features
✅ More than 2,000 questions
✅ 9 knowledge categories
✅ Easy and difficult questions
✅ Lifelines and assistance options
✅ Score and progress tracking
✅ Achievement rewards
✅ Review of incorrect answers
✅ Sound effects and interactive gameplay
✅ Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

🎯 Purpose

Milioner Znanja helps players expand their knowledge while enjoying a challenging quiz experience. The game is suitable for students, families, and anyone who loves learning through play.
